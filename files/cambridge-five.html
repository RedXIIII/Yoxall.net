THE STORY BEHIND THE HEADLINE

Kim Philby, Guy Burgess, Donald Maclean, Anthony Blunt and John Cairncross passed classified information to the Soviet Union. Their British education and careers gave them access to sensitive institutions. A 2025 MI5 file release adds interrogation transcripts, surveillance material and confessions to the established history.

“Spy ring” can imply a single continuously coordinated team. The National Archives cautions that the five took different paths and acted independently to a substantial degree, despite their connections. That makes the history more complex than the familiar label.

The new files show the evidential texture behind the story: interviews, intercepted conversations, memoranda and admissions. They support particular histories of access and betrayal. A person appearing in a released intelligence file is not automatically a spy; the same release includes investigations that established no cause for suspicion.
