THE STORY BEHIND THE HEADLINE

NASA’s 2023 independent study called for better calibrated observations, metadata and consistent reporting of unidentified anomalous phenomena. AARO’s 2026 public material includes an unresolved infrared recording whose description explicitly carries no analytical conclusion. Unidentified observations are real investigatory subjects; these sources do not establish extraterrestrial craft, extraordinary performance or a recovered alien technology programme.

A military recording can be authentic while its subject remains unidentified. A single sensor view may omit distance, scale, motion of the observing platform or information needed to distinguish a physical source from an imaging effect. NASA’s independent study therefore focused on how to gather evidence capable of answering the question, rather than treating the existence of an unexplained clip as the answer. Reliable metadata and simultaneous observations can transform an arresting image into a testable event.

AARO’s 2026 material includes PR49, a one-minute-and-49-second infrared recording with no oral or written account supplied by the reporter. Its descriptive notes explain changes in zoom and contrast and expressly avoid an investigative conclusion. That is a useful example of disciplined uncertainty: the release does not identify the cause, but neither does it demonstrate impossible acceleration or alien origin. A serious open file should explain what is missing and what new measurements would distinguish competing explanations.

Where the evidence stops.

Unresolved does not mean inexplicable, and missing evidence does not give every proposed explanation equal support. The cited releases do not establish alien craft or extraordinary physical performance.

What would move this forward?

  • Obtain calibrated multi-sensor observations with time, position and range information.
  • Model platform motion, perspective, zoom, contrast and known sensor artefacts.
  • Compare physical predictions of proposed explanations with independent measurements.
