GoFast: speed without the speed

The ocean races past. The object need not.

Speed claim resolved; object unidentifiedDiscuss in the Signal Room ↗Locate in the observation room ↗

The surviving observation.

GoFast appears to show a small object racing close to the sea. AARO's February 2025 analysis reached a different conclusion: the target was approximately 13,000 feet above the Atlantic off Florida, and the apparent rapid movement was affected by viewing geometry and parallax. Accounting for the wind, its assessed speed range was roughly 5 to 92 mph. AARO expressed high confidence that the object displayed no anomalous performance characteristics, while explicitly saying it could not definitively identify it. This is a resolution of the dramatic speed interpretation, not a complete identification of everything visible in the frame.

Resolved refers to the anomalous-performance claim. AARO's speed estimates depend on geometry, wind and heading assumptions; the object itself remains unidentified.
