Aguadilla: the apparent sea crossing

One object splits and dives—or two drift over land.

Motion resolved; likely sky lanternsDiscuss in the Signal Room ↗Locate in the observation room ↗

The surviving observation.

A Customs and Border Protection aircraft recorded infrared footage near Aguadilla that appeared to show fast movement, an object splitting, and a passage into the sea. AARO's reconstruction instead found two objects traveling together at wind speed over land, without entering the water. Its March 2025 report assessed with high confidence that there was no anomalous behavior or movement between air and water, and with moderate confidence that the targets were a pair of sky lanterns. The video remains fascinating as a demonstration of how an aircraft's changing position, distance and viewing angle can transform an ordinary track into a dramatic visual story.

Where the evidence stops.

AARO's reconstruction is an analytical model, not another camera view of the encounter. The probable sky-lantern identification is less certain than its finding about the objects' motion.
