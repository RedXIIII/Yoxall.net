The surviving observation.

An MQ-9 recorded an apparently silver, orb-like object crossing its field of view in the Middle East. AARO director Sean Kirkpatrick presented the clip at an open Senate hearing in April 2023. The official description is unusually clear about the boundary of the finding: AARO did not assess the object as exhibiting anomalous behavior, but could not identify it from the available material. It retained the case in its active archive pending additional information. The short recording is compelling because it preserves an observation while also showing how little a brief image can tell investigators about the thing being observed.
