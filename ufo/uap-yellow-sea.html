The surviving observation.

Released in the July 2026 PURSUE collection, this short infrared recording follows an area of contrast over the Yellow Sea. AARO's official description says that its appearance resembles a six-pointed star and that the sensor pans to keep it near the center of the display. The entry remains an unresolved UAP report. Crucially, its description is not an analytical determination of the target's validity, nature or significance. A distinctive sensor return is a reason to examine an image closely; it does not establish that the physical object has that shape, or that the recording shows unusual propulsion or flight performance.
