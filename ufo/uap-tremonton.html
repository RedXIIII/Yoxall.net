Tremonton: the surviving 1952 film

Seventy-four years later, the print still has limits.

The surviving observation.

In July 1952, Navy warrant officer Delbert Newhouse filmed bright airborne objects near Tremonton with a handheld 16 mm camera. The film entered the Air Force's UFO investigation record and was later examined in Project Blue Book. AARO's September 2026 release digitizes a surviving print, describing its limited detail and missing fixed reference points as obstacles to a conclusive resolution. The physical record has its own mystery: after approximately 55 seconds, it switches to unrelated sewing-equipment footage. AARO says the available record does not establish how the materials were combined. An official archival release preserves this uncertainty rather than solving the original sighting.

Where the evidence stops.

The digitization does not establish target distance, size, speed or origin. Its surviving print may be composite or reused; AARO does not establish when or how unrelated material was added.
