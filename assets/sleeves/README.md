# Planner FM sleeve collection

12 active song-inspired sleeves generated with the built-in image_gen tool from the user’s face references and supplied album art direction. Most sleeves feature the hooded MC. “The West Don’t Rest” opens the album from a stormy Cornish harbour, with slate roofs, Atlantic surf, heavy speakers and small repairs-planning details. Full-size web sleeves and smaller track-index thumbnails are served locally. “Cutter Is Go: Ctrl+Alt+Legend” uses the supplied suited-hero cover with a darker blue-black and amber colour grade, preserving Phillip’s face, pose and the original lettering. “Get Your TICs Out” brings the thermal camera, banjo and cowboy costume together with a novelty-bra prop gag. “Zoe: Here We Go Again (Unplugged)” features Zoe from her supplied photograph, with the hooded MC on acoustic guitar in a warmer office session. The main session’s house finale, “Phone Down, Bass Up”, features Amanda on the vinyl decks alongside the hooded MC in a mirrorball-lit house party, with the office jokes tucked into the booth. The penultimate bonus record, “Practically Perfect (For Michelle)”, features Michelle and her partner in a warm romantic coastal portrait, using both supplied face references. “Darren is a Bellend!” is the closing bonus record.

The exact prompts, generation metadata and original PNGs are preserved outside this publishing directory in `../../../research/artwork-source-v2/` relative to this folder. The private reference photographs are not published.

The track catalogue controls sleeve paths and running order. Keep every sleeve paired with its own song rather than assigning art by track number.
