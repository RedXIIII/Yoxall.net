# Planner FM sleeve collection

11 lyric-inspired sleeves generated with the built-in image_gen tool from the user’s face references and supplied album art direction. The MC wears a hood in each scene. Full-size web sleeves and smaller track-index thumbnails are served locally. “Get Your TICs Out” brings the thermal camera, banjo and cowboy costume together with a novelty-bra prop gag. “Zoe: Here We Go Again (Unplugged)” features Zoe from her supplied photograph, with the hooded MC on acoustic guitar in a warmer office session. The closing house track, “Phone Down, Bass Up”, turns the repairs office into an afterparty.

The exact prompts, generation metadata and original PNGs are preserved outside this publishing directory in `../../../research/artwork-source-v2/` relative to this folder. The private reference photographs are not published.

The track catalogue controls sleeve paths and running order. Keep every sleeve paired with its own song rather than assigning art by track number.
