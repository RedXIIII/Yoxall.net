Before you send

Your name, message and browser’s public signature are sent to independent public Nostr relays. Anyone may copy them. Deletion and continued availability cannot be guaranteed. Relays also receive your connection information, including your IP address.

A private signing key and drafts stay in this browser’s local storage. Clearing that storage changes your signature. Use a pseudonym and avoid personal details. This room does not verify names or provide private messages.

About this shared room

Messages use the open Nostr protocol and the public relays relay.damus.io, nos.lol and relay.nostr.net. At least one relay must acknowledge a signal before it is marked sent. If every relay fails, your draft remains here.

Muting hides an author in this browser only. It does not delete their public messages. No official archive judgement should be inferred from a visitor’s post.
