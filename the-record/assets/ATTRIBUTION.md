# The Record — image provenance

The photographs depict actual objects and sites. Document images are faithful renders of original government scans. All images are hosted locally; no generated images are presented as evidence.

- `egyptian-diorite-vessel.jpg`: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, diorite jar 2021.41.59, Bequest of Nanette B. Kelekian, 2020. Public Domain / CC0. [Object record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/329827); [Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access). Resized and compressed for the site; cropped in some displays.
- `giza-pyramids.jpg`: Ricardo Liberato, with Wikimedia Commons retouching by Ikiwaner. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:All_Gizah_Pyramids.jpg). [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/). Cropped and colour-treated through CSS; adapted displays retain the same licence.
- `antikythera-mechanism.jpg`: Peulle (Petter Ulleland), 2017. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Antikythera_Mechanism_(NAMA)_2017.jpg). [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Resized Wikimedia thumbnail; adapted displays retain the same licence.
- `mkultra-hearing.jpg`: Title page of the US Senate hearing, Project MKULTRA, 3 August 1977. US government publication, public domain. [Faithful government scan and provenance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ProjectMKULTRA_Senate_Report.pdf).
- `northwoods-memo.jpg`: Joint Chiefs of Staff memorandum, Justification for US Military Intervention in Cuba, 13 March 1962. US government record. [Original scan](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/Joint-Chiefs-pretexts-to-invade-Cuba-March-1962_0.pdf).
- `nsa-report.jpg`: Privacy and Civil Liberties Oversight Board, telephone-records report, 23 January 2014. US government publication. [Original PDF](https://documents.pclob.gov/prod/Documents/OversightReport/ec542143-1079-424a-84b3-acc354698560/215-Report_on_the_Telephone_Records_Program.pdf).

Document images were rendered at 700 pixels on the longest side and compressed as JPEGs. CSS crops, rotations and tonal adjustments are identified in the site's credits. Original source links and substantive evidence limits accompany each dossier.
